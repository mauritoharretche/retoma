# RETOMA roadmap

This roadmap describes planned work only. The estimates are forward-looking and do not represent completed work.

## Product interface — 5 hours

- Build accessible visual flows for creating, editing, archiving, and listing activities (5 hours).

## Resumption workflow — 8 hours

- Record the current stopping point, next action, and estimated duration (3 hours).
- Add time-available filtering and prioritised resume suggestions (3 hours).
- Cover the core business rules and user flows with tests (2 hours).

## Testing and accessibility — 6 hours

- Expand unit and integration tests for critical domain and UI paths (3 hours).
- Audit keyboard navigation, focus management, semantic structure, and accessible labels (2 hours).
- Add meaningful empty, error, and loading states where needed (1 hour).

## Data portability and resilience — 5 hours

- Design and implement safe export of local data (2 hours).
- Validate and import compatible exports with clear recovery errors (2 hours).
- Document local-data backup and restoration guidance (1 hour).

## PWA improvements — 5 hours

- Define offline-first requirements and installability scope (1 hour).
- Add an app manifest and offline asset strategy (2 hours).
- Test installed and offline behaviour across supported browsers (2 hours).

## Documentation and release preparation — 4 hours

- Document the local-only architecture and data model (1 hour).
- Maintain contribution, quality, and privacy guidance (1 hour).
- Prepare a release checklist and verify build artefacts (2 hours).

**Estimated future work: 33 hours.**
